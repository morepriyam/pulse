Record. Caption. Deliver.

Pulse is the fastest way to get a video from your phone into your organization's systems. Record a quick walkthrough, caption it on your device, and deliver it to your own server — no third-party cloud.

Built for getting video where it needs to go

No accounts. No sign-ups. Your organization runs the server — your content stays on your infrastructure.

🎬

Record in clips

Shoot in short clips instead of one long take. Reorder them, re-record just the one you fumbled, and import footage from Photos.

✂️

Trim without quality loss

Every edit works from the original. Clips merge into one clean video on export.

💬

On-device captions

Automatic captions generated entirely on your phone — audio never leaves the device. Edit them word-by-word in the built-in editor.

📦

Deliver to your server

Pair with your organization's server by scanning a QR code. Your video, captions, and thumbnail arrive as one bundle, ready to attach to a ticket, record, or wiki page.

📶

Resilient uploads

Uploads resume automatically after signal drops. Start it and forget it.

✈️

Works fully offline

Record, edit, and caption with no connection at all. Share drafts device-to-device as .pulse files.
